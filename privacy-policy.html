Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 1, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how this Discord bot (“the Bot”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use its features through Discord.

By using the Bot, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information Collected

The Bot may process: - Discord user IDs - Discord usernames and display names - Messages sent where AI features are enabled - Slash command inputs - Lore content voluntarily submitted by users - Reputation/star data - Timestamps related to stored content

The Bot does not intentionally collect passwords, payment information, government identification, or precise location data.

3. AI Features

AI responses are generated using a locally hosted Ollama instance running on hardware controlled by the Bot operator.

Messages used for AI conversations are not sent to third-party AI providers.

To provide conversational context, the Bot may temporarily retain approximately the last 20 messages of an active conversation. These messages are used only to improve response quality and are not intended to function as permanent chat logs.

Conversation context is periodically discarded and may also be cleared whenever the Bot restarts or when no longer needed.

4. Lore

Lore pages, edits, references, timestamps, and author/editor Discord IDs are stored so the lore system can function correctly.

Lore information remains stored until edited or deleted by authorized users.

5. Reputation

If enabled, reputation (“stars”) awarded to members may be stored together with the recipient’s Discord ID and related information necessary to maintain the feature.

6. Avatar Processing

Image-generation commands may temporarily download a user’s Discord avatar to create an image requested by a user. These images are processed in memory and are not intentionally retained after the command completes, except where Discord itself stores the resulting message or attachment.

7. How Information Is Used

Information is processed to: - Respond to commands - Operate AI conversations - Maintain lore - Maintain reputation systems - Improve reliability and security - Prevent abuse

The Bot does not sell personal information and does not use collected information for advertising.

8. Information Sharing

Information is not sold or shared with advertisers.

Information may be disclosed only: - through Discord when responding to commands, - to hosting infrastructure controlled by the Bot operator, - when required by law, - or when necessary to protect the security or integrity of the Bot.

9. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

10. User Rights

Users may request deletion of lore content or other stored information where reasonably possible by contacting the Bot operator.

11. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Bot evolves. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
